Official ROAS Computation Guide (Richmond Tan Framework)
Core E-Com Unit Economics
"High ROAS on your ads dashboard is a vanity metric if it is below your Break-Even threshold."
Scaling ad budgets without calculating your exact Break-Even ROAS causes store capital bleeding. Platform commissions, payment fees, shipping subsidies, packaging, and nonrecoverable digital advertising VAT (12%) must be deducted from revenue first before determining allowable ad spend.
1. Actual ROAS
ROAS = Attributed Rev ÷ Ad Spend

The campaign efficiency reported by Meta, TikTok, or Shopee Ads Manager.

2. Contribution Margin
CM = Contribution ÷ Net Revenue

Contribution = Net Selling Revenue − Variable Costs per order (before ads).

3. Break-Even ROAS
Base: 1 ÷ CM
12% Ad VAT: 1.12 ÷ CM

The minimum ROAS floor to avoid operating losses. If ROAS is lower, every order loses money.

4. Target ROAS
Target ROAS = 1.12 ÷ (CM − Desired Margin)

The required ads manager ROAS to bank your target net profit margin after all costs and taxes.

Official Worked Example from Guide (₱400 Sale)

Reference Model

Assuming a product selling at ₱400.00, with ₱200.00 product cost, and ₱150.00 total marketplace fees, packaging, and variable costs before ads:

Pre-Ad Contribution ₱50.00
12.5% Contrib Margin
Base Break-Even ROAS 8.00×
1 ÷ 0.125
12% Ad VAT BE ROAS 8.96×
1.12 ÷ 0.125
At 10.00× ROAS Profit ₱5.20 / order
Ad: ₱40 + ₱4.80 VAT = ₱44.80
Key Insight: At 10.00× ROAS, pre-VAT ad spend is ₱400 ÷ 10 = ₱40.00. Because 12% digital ad VAT is nonrecoverable, total ad cost is ₱40 × 1.12 = ₱44.80. The merchant nets ₱5.20 profit (₱50 − ₱44.80). At 8.00× ROAS without adjusting for VAT, the seller would lose ₱4.80 on VAT!
