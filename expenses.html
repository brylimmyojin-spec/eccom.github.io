Richmond Tan ROAS Framework & Unit Economics
Core E-Com Formula
"High ROAS on your ads dashboard is a vanity metric if it's below your Break-Even threshold."
As emphasized by Philippine e-commerce mentor Richmond Tan, scaling ad budgets without calculating your exact Break-Even ROAS causes store capital bleeding. Platform commissions, shipping subsidies, packaging, fulfillment labor, and BIR taxes must be deducted from revenue first before determining allowable ad spend.
1. Break-Even ROAS
BE ROAS = Selling Price ÷ Pre-Ad Profit

The absolute floor. If your ads manager ROAS is below this ratio, you are actively burning money on every sale.

2. Target ROAS
Target ROAS = Price ÷ (Pre-Ad Profit − Target Profit)

The campaign ROAS you must achieve in Shopee / TikTok Ads to pocket your full desired net profit margin (e.g. 20%).

3. Max Allowable Ad Spend
Max Ad Spend = Pre-Ad Profit − Target Profit

The maximum allowable cost-per-purchase (CPA) you can bid before eating into your target net profit margin.
