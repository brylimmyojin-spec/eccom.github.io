The Golden Rule Pricing vs. ROAS Connection

"Product price sets the rules, and ROAS plays the game."

ROAS itself is never included or permanently baked inside your product price. Use your pricing formula to find out what ROAS you need to survive.

Capital Protection Framework
1 Your Price & Costs Come First

Set your price based on what the product is worth and what it costs to produce and fulfill (COGS, freight, packaging, fees, and baseline net margin).

2 Profit Margin Sets Your Ad Limit

Subtract landed product costs and platform fees from selling price. Whatever money remains is your maximum allowable ad budget to acquire a buyer.

3 ROAS Tells You If Ads Can Win

Use ROAS in Ads Manager to verify if your campaigns stay safely inside that profit limit, rather than bleeding your working capital into the negative.

Richmond Tan Framework & Marketplace Pricing Guardrails
Strategic Reference
1. The Vanity ROAS Trap (Richmond Tan): Philippine sellers often celebrate an 8.0× or 10.0× ROAS in Meta Ads or Shopee Ads Manager, unaware they are actually losing money. Why? Because marketplace commissions, transaction fees, shipping subsidies, packaging, and especially the 12% nonrecoverable digital ad VAT eat into the gross margin. Your ROAS must exceed the 12% Ad VAT Adjusted Break-Even ROAS floor to avoid draining your working capital.
2. The Shopee Compounding Fee Trap: Never artificially inflate base SRP to cover high ad budgets on Shopee. Shopee's commissions (4%–5%+), transaction fees (2.24%), service fees (1.5%), and Mega Discount Vouchers (4%/5% up to ₱200 cap) are charged as a percentage of the total customer price. If you mark up SRP by ₱100 to pay for ads, Shopee takes ~₱15.50 in extra platform deductions on that markup alone, while cratering your conversion rate!
1. Actual ROAS
ROAS = Attributed Rev ÷ Ad Spend

The standard ratio reported in Ads Manager. It only compares top-line customer revenue to ad spend, completely ignoring product costs, seller fees, and taxes.

2. Contribution Margin (CM)
CM = (Take-Home − Landed Cost) ÷ Net Rev

What remains from every order after paying marketplace fees, taxes, product sourcing, freight, and packaging, but before ad spend.

3. 12% Ad VAT BE ROAS
BE ROAS = 1.12 ÷ CM

The minimum ROAS floor required to not lose money. Incorporates the mandatory 12% nonrecoverable digital advertising VAT paid on ad invoices.

4. Target ROAS
Target = 1.12 ÷ (CM − Desired Margin)

The required Ads Manager multiplier to bank your target net profit margin (e.g. 20%) in cold cash after all deductions, VAT, and ad costs.
