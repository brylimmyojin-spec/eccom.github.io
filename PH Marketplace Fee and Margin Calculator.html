Rates and limits

Marketplace seller rates for the Philippines, VAT-inclusive, from the Shopee, TikTok Shop and Lazada Seller Centers (Lazada commission effective 4 Jul 2025, TikTok commission Nov 2025, 2026 fee notices) and your Shopee commission sheet. Mandatory fees apply to every order. Opt-in programs cost extra: Shopee MDV is 4% (5% on spike days), TikTok MDP is 4% (5% on campaign days), both capped at ₱200 per item. Lazada Promo Pass (5.8%, ₱100 cap) is automatic for all sellers. *Shopee’s seller growth fee (1.5%) is an unverified placeholder. Lazada’s page labels the payment fee 3%, but its own sample works out to 2.24%, which is used here. Withholding tax is 0.5% (creditable). Non-VAT sellers pay 3% percentage tax. Not modeled: new-seller waivers, seller vouchers, affiliate and ad fees, Mall rates. Confirm in Seller Center before relying on a rate.
