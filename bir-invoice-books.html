BIR Sales Invoice & Books of Accounts Guide

Practical step-by-step reference on what to write in your Sales Invoice, Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ), and Cash Disbursements Journal (CDJ).

The #1 Golden Rule for E-Commerce BIR Recording

Your Gross Sales on your Sales Invoice and Books of Accounts is the Buyer's Listed Price minus Seller-Funded Discounts only. Never deduct platform commissions, transaction fees, ads, or withholding tax from your Sales Invoice amount. Platform fees are recorded separately as Business Expenses.

Part 1: What to Write in Your BIR Sales Invoice EOPT Act (RA 11976 / RR 7-2024)

Under the Ease of Paying Taxes (EOPT) Act (RA 11976 / RR 7-2024), the Sales Invoice is the primary proof of sale for goods. Official Receipts are now only supplementary documents.

1. When Must You Issue a Sales Invoice?

Non-VAT Registered Sellers

Issue a Sales Invoice for every single order worth ₱500.00 or more, or for any amount if the buyer requests one. For daily orders below ₱500 where buyers did not request an invoice, consolidate them at the end of the day or payout cutoff into one invoice labeled "Various Customers".

VAT Registered Sellers

Issue a Sales Invoice for every sale, regardless of the amount. Make sure VATable Sales and 12% Output VAT are properly broken down on the invoice.

2026 E-Invoicing Rule (RMC 98-2026)

Micro taxpayers (annual gross sales below ₱3M) are exempt from mandatory structured JSON e-invoicing and may continue issuing BIR-registered manual Sales Invoices. Small, Medium, and Large e-commerce taxpayers (≥ ₱3M) must transition to BIR-accredited e-invoicing by December 31, 2026.

2. Sample Scenario & How to Fill Out the Sales Invoice

Scenario: An item is listed at ₱1,000.00. You gave a ₱50.00 Seller Shop Voucher. The platform gave a ₱100.00 Platform Voucher, charged ₱180.00 in platform fees, and withheld ₱4.75 (0.5% EWT). Net bank payout is ₱765.25.
Your Registered Business / Trade Name
Registered Business Address, City/Province

SALES INVOICE
No. 000101
Date: Nov 24, 2026
Sold To: Juan Dela Cruz (or "Various Customers" for consolidated small B2C orders)
TIN: (Optional for B2C; required only if B2B sale ≥ ₱1,000 to VAT buyer)
Address: (Optional for B2C)
Qty Unit Item Description / Platform Order Reference Unit Price Amount
1 pc Wireless Desk Lamp (Order ID: #261124ABC123) 1,000.00 1,000.00
Less: Seller-Funded Voucher / Discount (50.00)
TOTAL SALES (GROSS AMOUNT DUE) ₱ 950.00
Note on Platform Vouchers & Fees: The ₱100.00 Platform Voucher is funded by the app and paid to you, so it stays inside the ₱950.00 Gross Sales. Do not subtract the ₱180.00 platform fee here. Under RR 6-2026 / RMC 112-2026, if withholding tax applies on a B2B/platform remittance, stamp/indicate "Less: Withholding Tax ₱4.75" separately without altering Gross Sales.

3. Quick Reference: What to Include vs. Exclude on Your Sales Invoice

Transaction Item Include in Sales Invoice? Reason / BIR Treatment
Listed Selling Price (₱1,000) YES (+) Base gross selling price of the merchandise sold.
Seller Shop Voucher (₱50) YES (−) Direct price reduction granted by the seller (netted to ₱950).
Platform-Funded Voucher (₱100) DO NOT DEDUCT Subsidized by the marketplace; seller still earns full ₱950.
Platform Fees & Ads (₱180) DO NOT DEDUCT Separate operating expense; record in Cash Disbursements Journal.
Buyer-Paid Shipping Fee EXCLUDE Pass-through courier fee collected and remitted by the platform.
Part 2: What to Write in Your BIR Books of Accounts Sample CRJ & CDJ Included

Keep your registered books updated at your registered business/home address. The required set of books depends on your chosen income tax option on your BIR Certificate of Registration (COR / Form 2303):

8% Flat Income Tax Rate 2 Books

Usually requires only 2 Books — Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ) and Cash Disbursements Journal (CDJ), or a simplified columnar book per your RDO registration.

Graduated Income Tax (OSD or Itemized) 4 Books

Requires 4 Books — Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ), Cash Disbursements Journal (CDJ), General Journal (GJ), and General Ledger (GL).

1. Sample Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ) — Recording Sales & Bank Payouts

Record every Sales Invoice issued (or daily/weekly batch summary matching your issued Sales Invoices). Using our ₱950.00 order example, here is how you record the transaction and reconcile why only ₱765.25 entered your bank:

Cash Receipts Journal (CRJ) — Sample Columnar Book
Period: Nov 2026
Date SI No. Customer / Platform Gross Sales (Credit) Platform Fees (Debit) CWT 0.5% (Debit) Cash in Bank (Debit)
11/24/26 000101 Juan Dela Cruz Shopee 950.00 180.00 4.75 765.25
11/24/26 000102 Various Customers TikTok 2,400.00 432.00 12.00 1,956.00
Total for the Day / Period 3,350.00 612.00 16.75 2,721.25
Reconciliation Tip: Notice how Debits (₱612.00 + ₱16.75 + ₱2,721.25 = ₱3,350.00) always equal Credits (Gross Sales ₱3,350.00). This proves to the BIR examiner why your bank deposits are smaller than your Gross Sales.

2. Sample Cash Disbursements Journal (CDJ) — Recording Expenses & Fees

Record all business cash outflows and platform charges supported by official Sales Invoices / Billing Statements from suppliers, couriers, and the e-commerce platform.

Cash Disbursements Journal (CDJ) — Sample Columnar Book
Period: Nov 2026
Date Ref / SI No. Payee / Supplier Particulars Purchases / COGS (Dr) Platform & Ad Fees (Dr) Packaging & Supplies (Dr) Cash Paid (Credit)
11/24/26 PL-8891 Shopee Philippines Commission & Txn Fees — 180.00 — 180.00
11/25/26 SI-4410 ABC Packaging Corp. Bubble wrap & pouches — — 650.00 650.00
11/26/26 SI-9921 XYZ Wholesale Trading Restock desk lamps 5,000.00 — — 5,000.00
Total for the Period 5,000.00 180.00 650.00 5,830.00
Avoid Double-Counting Fees: If you already deducted Platform Fees inside your CRJ (Method A — Net Payout Reconciliation), do not add the same fee again to your total deductible expenses from the CDJ. Alternatively (Method B), record full Gross Sales as Cash/Wallet in CRJ and log the monthly Platform Billing Statement (e.g., PL-8891) once in the CDJ. Stay consistent across the year.

3. General Journal (GJ) & General Ledger (GL) (For 4-Book Taxpayers)

Book Name What to Write Inside
General Journal (GJ) Non-cash transactions, returned/refunded orders (Credit Memos), quarterly application of BIR Form 2307 (0.5% withholding tax credits), depreciation, and year-end closing entries.
General Ledger (GL) Monthly summary totals transferred from your CRJ, CDJ, and GJ grouped by account title (e.g., Cash in Bank, Sales, Platform Commission Expense, Purchases, Creditable Withholding Tax).

Part 3: Tax Impact & Compliance Checklist

How Your Tax Option Treats Platform Fees

8% Flat Income Tax

Tax is computed as 8% of Gross Sales (in excess of ₱250,000). Platform fees cannot be deducted from your tax base.

Graduated Tax + OSD (40%)

Automatic 40% deduction from Gross Sales; actual platform fees cannot be deducted separately.

Graduated Tax + Itemized Deduction

Platform commissions, transaction fees, and ad spend are fully deductible as operating expenses, provided you download and keep the monthly BIR Sales Invoices/Billing Statements issued by the platform.

Monthly, Quarterly & Record-Keeping Checklist

Download Monthly Platform Tax Invoices

Always download the BIR-compliant Sales Invoice / Billing Statement generated inside Shopee, Lazada, or TikTok Seller Center for the fees deducted from your payouts.

Collect Quarterly BIR Form 2307

Download your quarterly Certificate of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source (0.5% EWT under RR 16-2023) from Seller Center and attach it to your quarterly/annual Income Tax Return (1701Q / 1701A / 1701) to reduce your tax payable peso-for-peso.

5-Year Record Retention

Under RR 7-2024, preserve all manual/electronic books, issued Sales Invoices, platform billing statements, and Form 2307 copies for at least 5 years from the filing deadline.
