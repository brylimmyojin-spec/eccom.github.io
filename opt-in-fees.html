Shopee FRS Opt-in & Opt-out Mechanics

  1. Login to Shopee Seller Centre PH
    Navigate to Marketing Centre > Campaign > Free Returns Service or click the direct portal link above (Campaign ID: 470000001000041).
  2. Review Eligible Categories & Terms
    Verify your store eligibility. Most non-perishable categories qualify automatically. Review the ₱2.00/order fee schedule.
  3. Click "Opt-in" & Activate
    Once confirmed, the program activates on your shop within 24–48 hours. The ₱2.00 fee only applies to orders placed after activation.
  4. Flexible Opt-out Anytime
    If your return rates drop or you wish to exit, you can submit an opt-out request through the same campaign link. Changes take effect on the next scheduled billing cycle.
Important: The ₱2.00 fee applies to all completed orders while enrolled, not just orders that are actually returned. Use the calculator above to ensure your return volume justifies the enrollment.
