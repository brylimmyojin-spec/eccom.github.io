Philippine E-Commerce Tax Guide

Understanding BIR regulations, withholding tax rules (RR 16-2023), and VAT vs. Non-VAT accounting.

Test in Pricing Simulator
1. BIR RR 16-2023 (0.5% WHT)
Creditable Withholding Tax

The Bureau of Internal Revenue requires e-marketplace operators (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok) to withhold 0.5% of gross remittances to online sellers.

  • Exemption: Annual gross remittances under ₱500,000 or qualifying BMBE accounts.
  • Credit Form: Marketplaces issue BIR Form 2307 quarterly, which you claim as a tax credit against your income tax liability.
Creditable against annual tax
2. Non-VAT Taxpayers (3%)
Percentage Tax (Sec. 116)

Small business owners whose annual gross sales do not exceed the ₱3,000,000 threshold generally file under the 3% percentage tax regime.

  • Pricing Rule: You do not break down output VAT on invoices.
  • Model Treatment: A 3% provision is deducted in the calculator as tax expense when pricing items.
  • No Input VAT: Non-VAT sellers cannot credit input VAT billed on marketplace fees.
Standard small seller regime
3. VAT-Registered Sellers (12%)
Output VAT & Input VAT Credits

Mandatory for sellers with annual sales exceeding ₱3,000,000 (or voluntary registration).

  • Output VAT Formula: Consumer prices in the PH are VAT-inclusive: Price × (12 ÷ 112).
  • Input VAT on Fees: Marketplaces charge 12% VAT on their commission and transaction fees. You can claim this as input VAT credit against your output VAT!
Eligible for input VAT credits

Frequently Asked Questions for PH Sellers

The 0.5% withholding tax is not a platform fee or an expense. It is a creditable withholding tax remitted directly to the BIR on your behalf. At the end of each quarter, Shopee, TikTok Shop, and Lazada issue a digital BIR Form 2307 (Certificate of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source). When filing your quarterly or annual income tax return (BIR Form 1701 or 1702), you deduct this amount directly from your final income tax payable.

Lazada bundles payment processing into a 2.24% payment fee (2.0% + 12% VAT). However, Lazada sellers typically enroll in Promo Pass which levies a 5.8% fee (capped at ₱100) to cover platform shipping discount subsidies and voucher visibility.

Because fees in Philippine marketplaces are interdependent (some fees are fixed percentages of price, others have caps like ₱100 or ₱200, and VAT is subtracted before margin is calculated), finding the exact price by algebra alone can be complex. The calculator uses a binary-search solver to determine the precise price down to two decimal places that guarantees your desired target net margin.

Many online sellers falsely assume their Break-Even ROAS is simply Selling Price ÷ Product Cost. In Philippine marketplaces, you must first subtract Output VAT (12/112) or Percentage Tax (3%), BIR 0.5% withholding tax, platform commissions, transaction fees, and shipping subsidies to find your true pre-ad gross profit. As e-commerce educator Richmond Tan emphasizes:
True Break-Even ROAS = Selling Price ÷ (Platform Net Payout − COGS − Packaging − Opex)
If your Shopee Ads or TikTok GMV Max dashboard reports a ROAS below this floor, you are losing money on every single conversion.
