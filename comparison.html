Marketplace Fee & Strategy Decision Matrix
Shopee High Volume & Heavy Campaigns

Highest organic GMV volume in PH. Strongest voucher participation via MDV (4% regular, 5% spike days capped at ₱200) and FRS RTS Protection (₱2 flat fee) to eliminate return logistics risk.

TikTok Shop Affiliate Power & Live Selling

Excellent impulse conversion. Base commissions are competitive, but factor in the Category Growth Service Fee (1.0%–1.5%) and creator sample/affiliate costs when scaling.

Lazada Direct Payout & Search Discovery

Payment transaction fee is standard 2.24% with no separate shipping platform percentage. Lazada Promo Pass automatically absorbs 5.8% (capped at ₱100/item) for free shipping vouchers.

Tax Strategy: VAT-registered sellers should claim 12% Input VAT against official marketplace tax invoices, and Non-VAT sellers can use BIR Form 2307 to offset the 1% withholding tax against their quarterly income tax.
