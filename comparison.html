Key Strategic Takeaways
  • TikTok Shop: Offers lower base commissions in select fashion/lifestyle categories, but watch for the Category Growth Service fee (1.0%–1.5%).
  • Shopee: Strong organic traffic and campaign lift, but shipping subsidies (5.6% up to ₱100) and service fee (1.5%) increase total deductions.
  • Lazada: Payment fee is 2.24% with no separate shipping platform percentage, but Promo Pass adds 5.8% (capped at ₱100).
  • Input VAT Benefit: VAT-registered sellers should always claim input VAT on marketplace fees to recover 12% of the deduction.
